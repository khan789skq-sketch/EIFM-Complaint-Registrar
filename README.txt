EIFM WCC & PPM - Updated Streamlit application

Deploy the entire folder contents to your Streamlit repository.
Original WCC template, All.xlsx, All-3.xlsx and EIFM logo are included unchanged.

PDF export requires LibreOffice available on the server.
For Streamlit Community Cloud, packages.txt installs LibreOffice.
Generated PDFs can be downloaded on mobile and shared through the phone's Share menu.

Normal WCC: original Word template, optional second-page Before/After photos.
PPM WCC: same Word template with 1st-4th PPM, no photos, selected equipment checklist PDF combined into one PDF.
PPM: PDF of selected equipment worksheets; OK/Not OK marks centered.

Note: Original Excel template files are not edited. The generated selected-equipment workbook is converted to PDF. LibreOffice conversion may render differently from Microsoft Excel on some devices.
